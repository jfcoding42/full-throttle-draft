FULL THROTTLE PHONE APP (PWA)

Important:
A PWA must be served from a web address to install properly; opening index.html directly from Files usually will not register the service worker.

Once hosted:
iPhone (Safari):
1. Open the app website in Safari.
2. Tap Share.
3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
4. Launch Full Throttle from the new icon.

Android (Chrome):
1. Open the app website in Chrome.
2. Tap the menu.
3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

The app is configured for standalone display, an app icon, offline caching, and saved draft state.
